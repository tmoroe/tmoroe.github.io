01 / about

Curious about how things
run under the hood.

>> Hello, I'm Thabang

I am a Big Data Engineer with a deep interest in technology and software development, and a lasting curiosity about how computing systems work under the hood.

I am particularly passionate about building custom data platforms on open-source technologies, designed from the ground up to be secure, compliant and tailored to the needs of the business.

I am solutions-oriented and do my best work on complex problems that have a direct, measurable impact on product and business outcomes.

>> How I can help you

I design, build and maintain reliable data platforms and pipelines, ensuring that data is well-governed, high-quality and readily available. I monitor for trends and inconsistencies that may affect business objectives, and I communicate these insights clearly to stakeholders so the organisation can make informed, data-driven decisions.

Working within Agile teams, I deliver customer-focused solutions iteratively, helping the team maintain focus on rapid, high-quality delivery.
